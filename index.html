Privacy Policy for Kodi Junction

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Kodi Junction is a board game for Android, published by the developer named on its Google Play page. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The app saves your settings, coin balance, unlocked items, equipped items and a record of purchases already delivered. This stays on your device. Android's automatic backup may include it in your own Google account backup, which you control in your device settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment under its own privacy policy, and we never see your card or bank details. The app receives only what it needs to deliver your purchase, such as the product and a purchase ID. It keeps that on your device.

Permissions

The app uses the internet permission only to reach Google Play for purchases and prices.

Children

Kodi Junction is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. You can remove any copy in your backup through your Google account settings. Items bought on Google Play stay linked to your Google account under Google's policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date. If a future version adds features such as ads or online play that use data differently, the policy will be updated first.
