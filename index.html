How to play

If you know Chowka Bhara, Ashta Chamma or Chauka Bara, you already know the feeling. Here are the Gujarati rules.

Throw the cowries

Count the shells that land open. All four closed is the big one.

1one open
2two open
3three open
4all open
8all closed
A 4 or an 8 earns another throw.
  • Race home. Move your four gotis around the outer track, then the inner track to the center.
  • Capture. Land on a rival goti to send it back. Your first capture unlocks the inner track, and every capture earns another throw.
  • Stay safe. Gotis on the X squares can't be captured.
  • Make a Jodko. Two of your gotis together can't be captured by a single goti.
  • Win. The first player to bring all four gotis home wins the game.

Questions players ask

About the game, the rules and playing with family.
What is Kodi Junction?

Kodi Junction is a free Android game of the traditional Gujarati board game played with cowrie shells (kodi). Each player races four gotis around a 5×5 cross-and-square board, captures rival gotis and tries to bring all four to the center home first.

Is it the same as Chowka Bhara or Ashta Chamma?

It is the same family of game. Chowka Bhara (Karnataka), Ashta Chamma (Andhra Pradesh and Telangana) and Chauka Bara are regional names for race-and-capture games played with cowries on a square board. Kodi Junction uses the Gujarati rules: a 5×5 board, throws of 1, 2, 3, 4 and 8, the Tohd capture rule and the Jodko pair. More on the regional names.

Can I play Kodi Junction offline?

Yes. Matches against the computer and pass-and-play games with 2 to 4 players on one phone work with no internet and no account.

How do I play online with friends?

Tap Play Online, create a private room and share the room code or invite link. Up to four players can join, and computer players fill any empty seats. You can also match with anyone online.

Is Kodi Junction free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play, with optional in-app purchases. The Pro Pass removes banner and between-match ads, and ads never appear during a match.

Which languages does it support?

Nine: English, Gujarati, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic and Japanese.
