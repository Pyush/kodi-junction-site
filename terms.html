Playing online

Online matches are for 2 to 4 players. You can play with friends in a room you share by code or invite link, or with anyone through Play Anyone.

Other players see the name and avatar you choose. Don't use a name that is offensive, impersonates someone or includes personal details such as a phone number. Names that break this rule may be replaced automatically.

Each turn is timed: 15 seconds to roll and 30 seconds to move. If time runs out, the game plays that turn for you. A player who leaves, or stays disconnected for 30 seconds, is out of the match. In a two-player match the other player wins; with more players, the rest play on.

When not enough people are online for Play Anyone, computer players fill the empty seats so you don't have to wait. Matches with friends in a room are only with the people who join it.

Play fairly: don't modify the app or use other tools to interfere with matches. We may end matches or block online play for anyone who breaks these rules.
